UNDER THE HOOD

A studio.
Not a black box.

Open-weight models, connected through a resumable serverless pipeline.

01

Brief → screenplay

Qwen2.5-7B extracts supplied facts and builds a scene-by-scene script. Source-bound offer clauses protect prices and deadlines. Whole-script checks and bounded repairs reduce repetition and invented claims.

Modal A10G · structured JSON
02

Words → narration

Kokoro-82M voices each scene. Measured speech duration sets scene lengths, with bounded tempo adjustments to reduce dead air without cutting words.

Modal T4 · measured audio timing
03

Scenes → imagery

FLUX.1-schnell generates individual scene images. Promotional scenes receive varied view directions. Exact products, logos and character consistency are not guaranteed.

Modal A100 · independent still images
04

Assets → your film

Remote FFmpeg adds stabilized pans, zooms and fades, then assembles narrated scenes. Frame count, duration, audio presence and decoding are checked before download.

Modal CPU · H.264 / AAC · 720p / 25fps

Before you press generate

Generation incurs charges in your own Modal account. No local inference or rendering is used. This studio makes animated still-image videos, not native moving-camera AI footage. Estimates aren't guarantees; human review remains essential. Knowledge videos are not automatically web-researched or source-verified. Add verified context to your brief.
